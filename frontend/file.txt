 Project Overview 
 The project is a modern, responsive E-Commerce Website designed to showcase products and allow customers to view product details and place orders directly through the website. The website will contain a visually attractive landing/home page, product catalog, individual product description pages, About Us and Contact pages, along with a simple order form. When a customer chooses to purchase a product, they will click the Buy Now button, which will open an order form. Once the customer submits the form, the complete order/customer information will be sent directly to the owner's WhatsApp number.
